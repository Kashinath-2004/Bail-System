# Statutory Legal Knowledge Sources

This module provides deterministic legal lookups and statutory mappings between the Indian legal codes:

## Primary Statutes Referenced

1. **The Indian Penal Code, 1860 (Act No. 45 of 1860)**
   - Historical penal code for India, governing crimes committed up to 30 June 2024.
   - Foundation for the 2010–2018 DDL e-Courts dataset.

2. **The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (Act No. 45 of 2023)**
   - In force from 1 July 2024.
   - Repeals and replaces the Indian Penal Code, reorganizing offences and penalties.
   - Concordance verified against Bureau of Police Research & Development (BPR&D) and Ministry of Law & Justice official concordance tables.

3. **The Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (Act No. 2 of 1974)**
   - First Schedule: Classification of Offences (Column 5: Bailable vs Non-Bailable; Column 4: Cognizable vs Non-Cognizable; Column 6: By what Court Triable).
   - Chapter XXXIII: Provisions as to Bail and Bonds (Sections 436, 436A, 437, 438, 439).

4. **The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (Act No. 46 of 2023)**
   - In force from 1 July 2024.
   - First Schedule: Classification of Offences under BNS.
   - Chapter XXXV: Provisions as to Bail and Bonds (Sections 478, 479, 480, 482, 483).

## Multi-Section Legal Precedence Rule
Under Indian criminal procedural jurisprudence, when an accused is charged under multiple penal provisions:
- If **any** of the offences is classified as **Non-Bailable** in the First Schedule, the statutory benefit of Section 436 CrPC / Section 478 BNSS (bail as a matter of right) is unavailable.
- The bail application must be adjudicated under judicial discretion (Section 437/439 CrPC or Section 480/483 BNSS).

## Ethical AI Constraint on Bail Timelines
In accordance with judicial principles:
- Bail timelines cannot and must not be simulated or fabricated as a predicted number of days or certainty of release.
- Timelines provided by Justice AI represent standard statutory procedural stages (filing, notice to IO/PP, production of case diary, arguments, order) to inform citizens of the legal process.
