Methodological Architecture

How the Two-Stage Hybrid Decision System Works

In criminal court systems, cases naturally cluster into two drastically divergent operational distributions: rapid dispositions (bail grants, compounding, plea bargaining, summary disposal within 24 months) and chronic delay (heinous crimes, multi-accused conspiracies, Sessions trials exceeding 36 to 60+ months).

Single monolithic models struggle with high error because minimizing squared error across both distributions results in severe under-prediction for delayed cases and over-prediction for on-time cases.

Our 3-Stage Pipeline:

  1. Stage 1: Model 1c (H2O Distributed Random Forest): Classifies whether filing will complete on-time (≤ 24 mo) or delayed (> 24 mo) with ~80% accuracy.
  2. Router (Consensus Threshold p ≥ 0.85): Assesses classification confidence. High confidence cases route to specialized regressors.
  3. Stage 2: Specialist Regressors (Model 2a / Model 2b): Model 2a specializes in subtle variance within on-time cases (RMSE 4.90 mo); Model 2b specializes in complex delays (RMSE 13.79 mo).
