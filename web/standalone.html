Methodology

Research Architecture Breakdown

STEP 1

Filing-Time Only Information (No Data Leakage)

Many past machine learning papers suffer from subtle data leakage by evaluating variables only decided after filing (e.g. judge transfers, interim rulings). The pipeline strictly strips all post-filing columns.

STEP 2

Distributed Random Forest Delay Classifier (Model 1c)

Trained on millions of subordinate court filings. Predicts binary on-time (<= 24 mo) vs delayed (> 24 mo) resolution with ~80% accuracy and 84.1% F1 score.

STEP 3

Two-Stage Specialized Duration Regressors (Model 2a & 2b)

Cases are routed to Model 2a (optimized for on-time timelines, RMSE ~4.90 mo) or Model 2b (optimized for long-tail backlogs, RMSE ~13.79 mo). Low-confidence cases fall back to a monolithic baseline.
