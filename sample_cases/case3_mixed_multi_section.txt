IN THE COURT OF SESSIONS JUDGE, INDORE, MADHYA PRADESH
Sessions Case No. 205 of 2018
Date of Filing: 05 August 2018
State of Madhya Pradesh versus Vikram Singh and 2 Others

COMMITTAL PROCEEDINGS / CHARGE SHEET UNDER SECTION 173 Cr.P.C.
Police Station: Palasia, District: Indore, State: Madhya Pradesh

NAMES OF ACCUSED:
1. Vikram Singh, S/o R. S. Singh (Accused No. 1) - In Judicial Custody
2. Amit Verma, S/o S. Verma (Accused No. 2) - On Regular Bail
3. Deepak Chouhan, S/o Mohan Chouhan (Accused No. 3) - On Regular Bail

CHARGES:
Offences under Indian Penal Code, 1860:
- IPC Section 341 (Wrongful restraint)
- IPC Section 323 (Voluntarily causing hurt)
- IPC Section 326 (Voluntarily causing grievous hurt by dangerous weapons)
- IPC Section 34 (Acts done by several persons in furtherance of common intention)

STATUS OF ACCUSED PERSONS:
Accused No. 1 Vikram Singh was arrested at the scene with an iron rod and remains in judicial custody. Accused No. 2 and 3 appeared before the Magistrate and were enlarged on regular bail with surety bonds.

BRIEF FACTS OF THE CASE:
On 12 July 2018, the accused persons formed a common intention and wrongfully restrained the complainant on AB Road, Indore. Accused No. 1 struck the victim on the left forearm using an iron pipe, resulting in a fractured ulna bone, while Accused No. 2 and 3 inflicted simple physical hurt with punches. The weapon was seized under Section 27 Evidence Act. The Magistrate committed the matter to the Court of Session for trial under Section 326 IPC.
