IN THE COURT OF JUDICIAL MAGISTRATE FIRST CLASS, LUCKNOW, UTTAR PRADESH
Criminal Case No. 412 of 2016
Date of Filing: 14 March 2016
State of Uttar Pradesh versus Rajesh Kumar Sharma

POLICE REPORT / CHARGE SHEET UNDER SECTION 173 Cr.P.C.
Police Station: Hazratganj, District: Lucknow, State: Uttar Pradesh

NAME OF ACCUSED:
1. Rajesh Kumar Sharma, S/o Late O. P. Sharma, Resident of Gomti Nagar, Lucknow (Accused No. 1)

CHARGES:
The accused is charged under Indian Penal Code, 1860:
- IPC Section 323 (Voluntarily causing hurt)
- IPC Section 506 Part I (Criminal intimidation)

STATUS OF THE ACCUSED:
Accused not arrested during investigation; appeared on notice under Section 41A CrPC; enlarged on personal bond by the investigating officer.

BRIEF FACTS OF THE CASE:
On 10 February 2016, a verbal altercation occurred between the complainant and the accused regarding a shared parking space in Hazratganj market. The accused allegedly engaged in physical scuffle causing simple blunt hurt to the complainant's arm and uttered threatening words. Medical report confirms simple injuries. Investigation concluded and charge sheet submitted for judicial trial.
