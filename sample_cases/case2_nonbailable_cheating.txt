IN THE COURT OF CHIEF METROPOLITAN MAGISTRATE, BENGALURU URBAN, KARNATAKA
Crl. Case No. 890 of 2019
Date of Filing: 22 November 2019
State of Karnataka versus Suresh Gowda and Another

POLICE REPORT UNDER SECTION 173 Cr.P.C.
Police Station: Indiranagar, District: Bengaluru Urban, State: Karnataka

NAMES OF ACCUSED:
1. Suresh Gowda, S/o Anand Gowda (Accused No. 1) - In Judicial Custody
2. Ramesh Patel, S/o K. Patel (Accused No. 2) - Absconding

CHARGES:
Offences under Indian Penal Code, 1860:
- IPC Section 420 (Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property)
- IPC Section 406 (Criminal breach of trust)
- IPC Section 34 (Acts done by several persons in furtherance of common intention)

STATUS OF ACCUSED:
Accused No. 1 is remanded to judicial custody and currently detained in Central Prison. Accused No. 2 is absconding and non-bailable warrants have been issued.

BRIEF FACTS OF THE CASE:
The complainant entered into an investment agreement with the accused persons for procurement of IT hardware components. The accused induced the complainant to transfer a total sum of Rs 48,00,000 across multiple bank transactions under the promise of high commercial returns. After receiving funds, the accused dishonestly misappropriated the amount and failed to supply hardware. Accused No. 1 was arrested following banking audit tracing.
